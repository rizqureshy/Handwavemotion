warming up…
idle zoom 100%
  • ☝️Point — a hand moves the on-page cursor
  • 🤏Pinch-tap (thumb+index, other fingers open) — click, or press the hovered key
  • ✊Grab & drag — scroll the page like grabbing paper
  • 🤏🤏Both hands pinch & stretch — zoom the page in/out
  • ✌️Hold peace sign — summon / dismiss the keyboard
Works on regular web pages (not chrome:// pages or the Web Store). Keep the panel open while you browse — it is the camera host.
